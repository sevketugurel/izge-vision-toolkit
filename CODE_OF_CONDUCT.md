# Code of Conduct

İzge adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) by reference (CC BY 4.0, © Contributor Covenant).

In short: be respectful, accept constructive criticism, focus on what is best for the project, and do not harass, insult, or publish others' private information. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this and may ban repeat offenders.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact on the GitHub profile [@sevketugurel](https://github.com/sevketugurel). Reports are handled confidentially.

Enforcement guidelines follow section "Enforcement Guidelines" of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
